# Tamagotchi
<br>
## About
Our hackclub Tamagotchi game! This is our 2nd submission to [hackclub thirdspace](https://thirdspace.hackclub.com) where you spend 10 hours a week for 8 weeks making projects! We are only begginers, so please don't judge us too harshly. Something that might seem like it could've taken only half an hour might take us hours including debugging, fixing stuff, cleaning up scripts, customisation, and more.

What is a Tamagotchi game you might ask? It's basically like a virtual pet you need to look out and care for! You're meant to feed it, water it, and do tasks to raise it.
<br>
Made with ❤️ by [Smallz](https://github.com/smallzdev) and [Vick-St3r](https://github.com/Vick-St3r)
